# Licence for the combined dataset

`trials.csv` and `source_summary.csv` are adapted from four datasets published on
Zenodo under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They are shared under the same licence.

Changes made: columns renamed and mapped to a common schema, trials derived from
movement logs (Liu), empty and summary rows dropped (Mayor), age codes re-mapped
(Tzouras), and metadata added. Each row names its source in `source_citation`.

- Liu, Zhi (2024). Experiment data of VR way-finding research. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10784483
- Mayor, J., Alegre Landaburu, J., Lopez Fernandez, D., Arranz Paraiso, S. (2026). Spatial memory in Partially non-euclidean Virtual Reality (Dataset). https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18481749
- Kumar, Aman (2026). Anonymized Dataset for a QR-Assisted Indoor Navigation Framework Integrated with Koha. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23080208
- Tzouras, Panagiotis G. (2025). Perceived safety and first/last mile mode choices. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14967130

The original authors do not endorse this compilation.
